OWNER REQUEST / DECISIONS
-------------------------
Promote the owner's other game through the supplied Google Play developer page,

IMPLEMENTED POLICY
------------------
Android only; launch at15November2026 00:00IST
  2026-11-15T00:00:00+05:30 / 2026-11-14T18:30:00Z.
The date uses the phone's clock. There is no QA bypass or remote/server-time
check. Unit tests move a test clock; no emulator/phone clock was changed.

At most TWO reserved/displayed invitations per installation, shared by both
Player and Manager modes, every career and account. Reserve each impression
durably BEFORE display. Wait at least48hours after the previous reservation:
e.g.15November10:00IST -> earliest second17November10:00IST. Not the next day.
After two dismissals/appearances, never automatically offer again on that
installation. An interrupted display consumes its reservation conservatively.
Opening the Play link records a permanent stop before handing off to the OS;
it need not wait for a second dismissal. A failed link can be retried explicitly
in the current card but cannot cause future automatic invitations.

No installed-app detection. Installing/having the other game does not reset or
increase the cap. No claim is made that installation was detected or confirmed.
The cap survives normal updates/restarts, career switches, guide replay and
ordinary settings changes. Uninstall/reinstall or explicit full local-data
deletion clears installation-local history; this is NOT an account-wide cloud
marketing register or cross-device tracking system.

WHERE / WHEN IT APPEARS
-----------------------
Only Player Home or Manager Home, after at least five completed matches and
onboarding/settings hydration. Deferred for guides, offers, newspapers, rewards,
achievements, contracts, calendar simulation and the Player Home-return ad.
Shares the existing lowest-priority global modal queue. Never added to a live
match, result, career creation or checkout route. No background notification/job.

Respect permanent VIP, timed ad removal and active legacy premium. This publisher
recommendation is suppressed for ad-free users; their paid benefit is not changed.
If focus/foreground/eligibility is lost, an already-shown appearance ends rather
than popping back up. Foreground/Home entry refreshes history for a later eligible
visit. One launch-boundary timer (with native timeout clamping), no polling loop.

The existing rating invitation takes priority: Android snapshots whether rating
was previously requested BEFORE enabling the rating component. A first rating
ask cannot be immediately followed by promotion during that same Home visit.
The campaign can be considered at a later Home entry/foreground. The existing
five-match/rating policy is otherwise unchanged. iOS routes directly to the
original PlayRatingPrompt/system-review component; no new Apple promo/lifecycle.

STATE / FAILURE HANDLING
------------------------

Shape: version1, impressions0..2, lastShownAt (milliseconds/null), stoppedboolean.
Independent of settings, career envelopes, save schemas, account backup and IAP.
Malformed/unreadable state suppresses the invitation rather than resetting it.

Opening errors remain inline and dismissible; rapid taps launch once.
