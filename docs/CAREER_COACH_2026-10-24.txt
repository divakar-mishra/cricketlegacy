IMPLEMENTATION
--------------
- CareerGuide.tsx owns a scene-local, measurable highlight/arrow/card. The scrim
  does not intercept the actual control. GuideTarget anchors real native Views;
  GuideScrollView reveals targets in the nearest scroll container.
- Career chapters/copy are in src/content/careerGuides.ts; pure progress/layout
  helpers and UI-only action signals are in src/game/coachGuide.ts.
- Welcome offers Guide me / Explore on my own. No forced slideshow or timer.
- Player creation covers name, country, portrait, role, the actual attribute +,
  remaining points, club choice, review and saving. Points are never auto-spent.
- Home explicitly exposes Customise player and Training. Individual Player and
  Manager tabs have their own pointers, along with the actual next cricket step.
- Player Training explains sessions, focus, exact Coins cost, optional Train and
  Development Centre. Manager Training explains focus, workload/Recovery, saving
  and player overrides; it never presents Player session spending as club training.
- Match setup explains conditions, toss and Watch/Key/Instant. Live guides point
  to the real personal bowling plan, batting approach, score and Manager tactics.
  A visible live guide pauses simulation; leaving/finishing releases its pause.
- Back, Skip step, Skip guide and Show screen guide are available. Settings
  Replay game guides restarts onboarding/progress. Progress uses existing
  dismissedTips keys coach-v1:<chapter>:<target>; mode skip is independent for
  common/player/manager. No persisted schema change or new analytics.
- Guides yield to higher-priority queued dialogs; ordinary native dialogs are
  never preempted. No background-route coaching over match results.
- No forced victory, fake reward, paid checkout, selection boost or new grant.

CONCRETE BOWLING REGRESSION FIX
-----------------------------
canBowl previously required every player to have bowling mean >=25. Unallocated
youth bowling skills are18, so genuine BOWLER/ALLROUNDER players with a bowling
style could be excluded before the existing preferred-role spell logic ran.
Those two bowling roles now enter the eligible pool when they have a style.
Part-timers still require25; no style is still ineligible. Actual ability, XI,
captaincy, legal over limits and innings length remain authoritative. This does
not guarantee every innings allows a full spell, selection, wickets or a win.
Regression tests cover both roles at18, no-style/part-timer guards, >=12legal
balls in the full-length fixture, auto/live parity and unchanged skill values.
This is a reproducible bug path, not proof of the exact cause of the owner's
earlier match without that save's full state.
