WHY THE ENERGY BUGS EXISTED
Normal refresh/load and Gem refills implicitly defaulted to36. A separate
VIP-aware refresh helper was orphaned. Ad reward used the match base4 and
Math.min(36,current+reward), capable of reducing VIP overflow. Wallet text and
canonical resource-view capacity used separate hard-coded/cached indicators.
Focus-only UI refresh lacked foreground/point-boundary handling. Earlier pacing
tests assumed8minute matches and/or flat4cost, missing rapid/high-stage pain.
The historical diagnostic was captured before this fix; its defect assertions
are not desired acceptance criteria. Current source regression tests cover the
correct behavior instead. No schema shape or version change/reset is required.
