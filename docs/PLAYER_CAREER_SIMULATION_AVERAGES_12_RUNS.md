This is the latest accepted Player regression population from 29 August 2026:
four Batters, four Bowlers and four All-Rounders. Every save ran on Normal from
age 16 to retirement at 33, used Instant Sim, spent only earned Wallet Coins on
training, and used no IAP or rewarded ads.

Counting statistics are arithmetic averages per completed career. Batting
average, strike rate, bowling average, economy and decisive team win rate are
pooled rates calculated from the four saves in that role:

- batting average = total runs / total dismissals;
- strike rate = total runs / total balls × 100;
- bowling average = total runs conceded / total wickets;
- economy = total runs conceded / total balls bowled × 6; and
- decisive win rate excludes draws and no-results.
