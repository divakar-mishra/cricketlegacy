# Player Career Progression

The Career Path is the actual competition ladder. The profile tier (Academy Prospect, Domestic Regular, Franchise Star, International, Living Legend) is a separate status label and does not move the player between competitions.

## Competition ladder

1. **Grade A Cricket (start age 16)**
   - Six T20 matches against a complete seven-team Grade A competition.
   - Training attributes are capped at 62 and baseline opponent quality is 34.
   - Standard Specialist promotion: at least 5 appearances and 68% readiness, or 62 OVR fast-track.
   - There is no automatic Under-19 place. An earned promotion goes to Under-19 through age 18, or directly to a senior Domestic contract from age 19 onward.

2. **Under-19 (merit selection, through age 19)**
   - Eight player fixtures in a complete nine-team competition: four 50-over matches, then four T20 matches.
   - Training attributes are capped at 76 and baseline opponent quality is 48.
   - Standard Specialist promotion: at least 6 appearances and 68% readiness, or 65 OVR fast-track.
   - The player gets one Under-19 World Cup selection opportunity at age 18. Selection requires 3 actual regional Under-19 appearances, a 6.0 average rating and 55% role-aware readiness.
   - A selected player enters a playable six-country 50-over knockout: two quarter-finals, two semi-finals and a final. Two seeded countries receive quarter-final byes, so the player can play two or three World Cup matches.
   - World Cup merit is retained if an exceptional player earns a Domestic promotion before age 18. If the player is still Under-19 when selected, an earned Domestic move waits only until elimination or the final.
   - Under-19 World Cup matches use the normal Matchday flow and youth national XI. They never grant senior caps or alter the senior international tournament state.
   - The age floor awards a senior domestic contract at 20.

3. **Domestic Professional**
   - The player joins a domestic club for List A and First-Class cricket and
     holds a separate franchise affiliation for the T20 block.
   - Two or three rival domestic clubs can approach at the season boundary.
     Those offers are driven by domestic appearances and output, not by caps.
   - Domestic and franchise agreements can be held together. Changing one does
     not cancel the other, and national duty only overrides the clashing fixture.
   - Selection uses ability, form, coach trust, condition and format readiness. An established player can be left out.
   - Good match output raises national reputation; quiet performances can reduce it.
   - The normal strong-player call-up route opens from age 22 at 68 OVR and 80 national reputation. Exceptional players can debut from age 20 at 65 OVR and 95 reputation; the Late Bloomer route opens at age 27 and requires 70 OVR plus 80 reputation. Every route remains merit-led.

4. **International**
   - The first call-up locks the declared country and opens international tours and tournaments.
   - Selection is reassessed from current form, fitness, ability and reputation immediately before every bilateral tour or tournament. One early-season decision cannot lock the player out for the whole year.
   - A fully selected player can play 10 Tests, 25 ODIs and 15 T20Is in a successful season. Selection is still reassessed assignment by assignment, so injury, poor form, rest or tournament elimination reduces that total naturally.
   - April and May are protected for franchise cricket with no international fixtures. Outside that window, domestic cricket continues whenever the player is not selected; national duty wins only an actual calendar clash.
   - International captaincy, records, trophies and long-term legacy goals continue beyond the first cap.

5. **Retirement**
   - The player may choose retirement from age 33.
   - The game recommends retirement between 35 and 38 according to current form, fitness and selection outlook; every career receives the normal review by 38.
   - The career closes at age 40 at the latest.

## Kit sponsorship

- One earned kit-partner slot unlocks after the first selected-XI senior Domestic appearance. Grade A, Under-19 and Under-19 World Cup matches do not unlock or pay it.
- The three guaranteed Domestic-rate choices are All formats (16 paid appearances at 110 coins), White ball (10 at 180) and Red ball (six at 300). Each starts with a 250-coin signing bonus.
- The rate is multiplied once at signing: Domestic `1.00`, Franchise `1.25` at 68+ uncapped OVR, International `1.60` after a cap, and Icon `2.00` after a cap plus either 40 caps or 82+ OVR. Values round to the nearest ten and never change during that contract.
- A matching selected appearance pays Wallet Coins once. The contract ends at its quota or after two season rollovers, whichever comes first.
- A separate ₹499 permanent sponsor adds one extra slot to one exact Player save. It appears after sponsorship unlocks and pays 250/350/500/650 Wallet Coins per qualifying UTC week as stature rises. Production checkout stays disabled until a server verifies and binds each consumable transaction to its save.
- Story-event endorsements are separate off-shirt campaigns. Every active campaign keeps its original per-match payout and form/integrity/season expiry, pays idempotently once per played match, and never occupies or appears in either kit-partner slot.

## Paid training progression

- Grade A provides eight sessions per season with no more than four in one focus; Under-19 provides 12/six; senior professional cricket provides 18/nine.
- A session starts at 300 Wallet Coins and rises by 150 for every prior session that season. Current OVR then applies a `1.00x`, `1.25x`, `1.75x`, `2.50x` or `3.50x` cost band at below 70, 70-79, 80-84, 85-89 and 90+ respectively.
- Each completed session directly raises up to the three weakest eligible attributes in that focus by one to three points. This is not the Player Creation screen's point-conversion model.
- All-Rounders retain a smaller dual-discipline workload allowance (`1.25x` in Grade A/U19 and `1.55x` as seniors), followed by a `0.90x` role workload factor. This keeps engaged careers near the specialist 87-88 OVR range without hiding or cancelling any paid-session gain.
- The result card shows every attribute's exact old and new value, total direct growth and decimal OVR progress. Rounded OVR therefore never makes a successful session look wasted.
- A capped focus is disabled before payment. It cannot charge coins or consume a session without producing at least one direct gain.
- Training Accelerator gives `1.50x` gains for the next three paid sessions. The standard coin price, stage limits and focus limits still apply.

## Youth readiness

Youth readiness is 75% role-weighted output and 25% average match rating.

- Grade A output target: 140 runs for batters or 9 wickets for bowlers.
- Under-19 output target: 320 runs for batters or 16 wickets for bowlers.
- All-rounders use both disciplines, weighted toward their stronger output.
- Every selected-XI appearance counts toward the minimum-match gate, including a batter not required in a short chase. Runs and wickets remain zero when the player does not bat or bowl.

Archetype changes the youth route:

| Archetype      | Readiness | Match adjustment | Fast-track OVR adjustment |
| -------------- | --------: | ---------------: | ------------------------: |
| Prodigy        |       62% |               -1 |                        -3 |
| Specialist     |       68% |                0 |                         0 |
| Comeback Story |       67% |                0 |                        +1 |
| Late Bloomer   |       72% |               +1 |                        +5 |

These are merit gates, not automatic promotions. Match ratings contribute only 25% of readiness, so a high rating without enough runs or wickets cannot qualify. Grade A never grants an automatic Under-19 place; the Under-19 age safeguard still moves a player to Domestic at 20.

## Profile status labels

- Academy Prospect: below 58 OVR and not capped.
- Domestic Regular: 58-67 OVR and not capped.
- Franchise Star: 68+ OVR and not capped.
- International: at least one international call-up.
- Living Legend: capped and any one of 8,000 career runs, 300 wickets, 4,000 runs plus 150 wickets, five league titles, 60 caps, or 40 caps with 84+ OVR. A direct Legend entitlement can also grant this status.
