## Full description

```text
BUILD YOUR CRICKET LEGACY

Cricket Legacy is a single-player cricket career game where every season, selection decision and match builds a history that is yours. Start as a young Grade A cricketer and earn your way towards domestic and international cricket, or step into the manager's office and shape a club across multiple seasons.

Choose the role you want to live. Build a player career through performances and training, or control squads, tactics, transfers, facilities and matchdays as a club manager. Both careers use the same cricket simulation.

PLAYER CAREER

• Create your cricketer, role, batting hand and bowling style.
• Begin at age 16 in Grade A cricket and earn selection through real performances.
• Progress through youth, domestic, franchise and international opportunities.
• Train batting, bowling, fielding, fitness and mental attributes.
• Experience selections, contracts, injuries, form and milestones.
• Follow separate records across cricket formats and competitions.
• Build a life beyond the boundary through sponsorships and personal finance.

MANAGER CAREER

• Take charge of a fictional cricket club and guide it across seasons.
• Select the playing XI, batting order, captain and vice-captain.
• Set tactics and prepare for different formats, opponents and conditions.
• Recruit players through the transfer market and develop future talent.
• Manage staff, training, recovery and the youth academy.
• Improve facilities and the club stadium.
• Balance club finances, sponsorships, wages and matchday decisions.

THREE WAYS TO EXPERIENCE MATCHDAY

Watch ball-by-ball action, focus on key moments, or use Instant Sim when you want to move quickly. Every mode uses the same cricket engine, so presentation does not change the underlying odds or rewards.

Follow the score, phase, required rate, commentary and tactical decisions. Completed matches include batting and bowling scorecards for every innings.

A CRICKET WORLD THAT REMEMBERS

Cricket Legacy tracks fixtures, results, tables, form, records, rankings, trophies and milestones across your save.

Player Career separates Grade A, Under-19, domestic and international records. Manager Career maintains club results, league history, transfers, facilities and leadership choices.

BUILD YOUR OWN APPROACH

• Specialize as a batter, bowler, all-rounder or wicket-keeper batter.
• Adjust batting intent, bowling plans and field settings.
• Respond to form, fitness, morale, conditions and opposition strength.
• Develop players through training and long-term squad planning.

FICTIONAL CRICKET, MEANINGFUL DECISIONS

Teams, players, sponsors and competitions are fictional, giving each career room to create its own rivalries, records and turning points.

The game is for cricket fans who enjoy long-term progression, scorecards, strategy and career stories: earn a place, build a club and see the consequences unfold across years.

ACCESSIBLE WAYS TO PLAY

• Dark, light and system themes.
• Multiple graphics-quality settings for performance and battery control.
• Optional sound, music, haptics and notifications.
• First-run walkthrough, career guides and a Cricket Academy handbook.
• Optional usage analytics and crash reporting, both disabled by default.
• In-app Privacy Policy, feedback access and account-data controls.

FAIR PROGRESSION

Core Player and Manager careers can be played without a purchase. Optional items may accelerate progress, add currency or unlock cosmetic and convenience benefits, while career continuation remains available through normal play. Prices and availability are supplied by Google Play for your region.

CREATE THE CAREER YOU WANT TO REMEMBER

Will your player rise from Grade A cricket to the international stage? Can your manager turn a lower-tier squad into a lasting club? Start a save and build your legacy one season at a time.
```

## Play Console actions

1. Update the default English listing title and short description.
2. Paste the full description and verify its final character count in Console.
3. Run a Store Listing Experiment on the short description and first three
   screenshots rather than assuming one ASO version will convert best.
4. Review the conversion rate under **Grow users → Store presence**.
5. Localize only after the English listing and screenshots are stable; do not
   publish machine-translated claims without review.
