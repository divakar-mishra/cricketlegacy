IMPLEMENTED IN SHARED SOURCE
----------------------------
1. Player/Manager creation opts into session-local coach progress. A new form
   starts at its first step regardless of previous completion/skip keys. Back
   between Player's form pages retains progress within that setup. Skip guide
   opts out for this setup, replay works, and existing persistent Home/training
   guides retain their prior behavior. No global settings/save wipe or migration.
2. Player order: Name -> Country -> Gender -> Skin tone -> Portrait -> Role ->
   Career identity/styles -> Next/attributes. Name/country/gender/skin tone/
   portrait/role remain guarded by current field readiness, not historical keys.
3. Eight tone swatches have their own live target and explicit-choice state.
   Tone filtering precedes face choice. Changing tone invalidates the previous
   portrait choice; changing gender invalidates tone and portrait. Manual
   portrait, Randomize and Reset still work outside the strict spotlight;
   Randomize/Reset explicitly select both tone and portrait. Ordinary cosmetic
   editing is unchanged and does not acquire required creation gates.
4. Docked creation locks short-field scrolling. Tall portrait/club/style lists
   can scroll only within the current target; dimmed unrelated areas consume
   touches. Skip guide releases the restriction. Standard overlay career guides
   remain non-blocking. Native anchor callbacks read current step state to avoid
   a stale unlocked reveal while changing steps; out-of-date measurements are
   rejected. Highlight clipping accounts for partially scrolled targets.
5. Coach Back/Next/Skip remain outside the scrolling text area. Overflowing copy
   can still scroll for accessibility. Keyboard-shrunken coach height is not
   saved as its preferred height after keyboard dismissal.
6. Styles have a real target containing career identity, Ironman and batting/
   bowling hand/style controls. The follow-up attribute target includes level,
   difficulty, budget and every actual allocation control. Creation club/review
   targets no longer use short focusHeight crops that could mask real choices.
