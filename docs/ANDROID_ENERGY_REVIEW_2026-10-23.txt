RECOVERY AND PRESENTATION
1. PurchaseScreen.onWatchEnergyAd grants4 after a completed available rewarded
   ad, not the upcoming fixture's cost. From zero, an international Test needs
   three successful ads absent regeneration. Availability is not guaranteed;
   this is not a recommendation to weaken age/consent or ad-failure guards.
2. Gem refill costs10Gems. The paid Mental Coaching Session is a different
   product with form/confidence benefits. Do not describe it as a mandatory
   purchase: waiting is free and the store also exposes ad/gem alternatives.
3. CareerHub REFILL_ENERGY -> showShortageOffer -> form_recovery. Dialog actions
   are Not now / View option; View option opens the paid product. Free waiting
   is mentioned but lacks a direct action or recovery ETA. This can feel like
   a paywall even though free recovery exists. No new screenshot audit claimed.
4. CareerHub refreshes energy on navigation focus. No periodic energy refresh
   was found there or in PurchaseScreen, and App.tsx's foreground handler does
   not explicitly refresh energy. PurchaseScreen's one-second timer updates
   the starter-offer countdown only. Remaining on these screens can leave
   wallet/display/gating stale until an explicit refresh/navigation occurs.
   This is a source-flow finding, not a new physical-device reproduction.
