# Cricket: Player and Manager — Play Store creative brief

Give the prompt below to an image and video designer or generation tool. Attach the current [app icon](store-assets/app-icon-512.png), [feature graphic](store-assets/feature-graphic-1024x500.png), [listing copy](PLAY_STORE_LISTING.md), and selected real screenshots and footage from this folder. The current screenshots predate the rename; capture fresh screens where a needed feature is missing or old branding appears.

## Copyable production prompt

```text
Create a Google Play marketing set for the Android game “Cricket: Player and Manager”. Its phone icon label is “Cricket”. Use the attached approved app icon and feature graphic as identity references. Keep the actual app icon unchanged.

Game truth: This is a fictional, single-player cricket career and management game with two modes. In Player Career, start at age 16 in Grade A cricket, choose a playing role, train, earn selection through performances, reach youth/domestic/international opportunities, manage form and injuries, hire specialists or a physio, use equipment, negotiate sponsors, explore personal finance and off-field ventures, and build records over a long career. A free in-game adviser provides contextual suggestions. In Manager Career, select a club and XI, appoint leaders, set tactics and field positions, scout and recruit players, run training and recovery, develop staff and youth prospects, improve facilities and the home ground, manage club finances and progress through seasons. Both modes include match decisions, watch/key-moments/instant simulation, scorecards, press or story moments, rankings, trophies, achievements and Hall of Fame records. Show these through real screens only, and check the current build before claiming a feature or outcome.

Goal: Make the first three screenshots instantly communicate (1) cricket match action, (2) Player Career, and (3) Manager Career. Make viewers curious about the decisions and long-term progression without making the screenshots look unlike the actual app. Across the remaining images, reveal the game's depth. Use the existing deep green, charcoal, cricket white and warm gold identity. Strong contrast and one short headline per image; readable at phone-thumbnail size. Let genuine app UI occupy most of each screenshot.

Deliverables:
1. Eight individually exported portrait phone screenshots, exactly 1080 × 1920 PNG, in the order below. Use fresh captures from the current build when the supplied pack lacks a suitable screen. Preserve real text, controls, scores and results. Crop or reframe; never stretch a screenshot or generate replacement UI.
2. One 1024 × 500 PNG feature graphic using the approved image, with the exact app name “Cricket: Player and Manager”. You may propose one alternate composition, but keep the cricket/player/manager positioning and exact spelling.
3. A 45-second portrait 1080 × 1920 preview video edited mainly from real Android screen recordings. Provide the MP4, a clean thumbnail, a caption file and a shot list identifying each source clip. If a clip is missing, list the screen that needs recording instead of inventing footage.
4. A contact sheet of all eight screenshots and a review sheet listing every claim or visual that needs verification against the latest build.

Screenshot story, in order:
01 MATCHDAY — “Make every match count.” Lead with the live pitch, readable score and a meaningful match moment.
02 PLAYER CAREER — “Rise through the cricket ranks.” Show the Player Career home/progression and a real selected player.
03 MANAGER CAREER — “Build the club. Lead the XI.” Show Manager home or squad, with real team decisions visible.
04 TACTICS — “Set the field. Shape the match.” Show actual field placement or match preparation.
05 DEVELOPMENT — “Train for the next chance.” Show role-specific training, coaching, form or injury recovery. Use one real screen or a clean two-panel composition.
06 CLUB BUILDING — “Scout talent. Grow your ground.” Show scouting/transfers and a facility, academy or home ground using real captures.
07 LIFE BEYOND CRICKET — “Your career goes beyond the pitch.” Show Player Life, adviser, sponsors or investments accurately; do not suggest real-money returns.
08 LEGACY — “Every season leaves a record.” Show real records, rankings, achievements, press moments or Hall of Fame. Do not present QA-staged results as an ordinary career.

Preview video timeline:
00–04s: Open on genuine live gameplay and a decisive ball or result. On-screen text: “Two careers. One cricket world.”
04–12s: Player creation/home, training and career progression. Caption: “Earn your place.”
12–20s: Manager squad, XI, tactics and field. Caption: “Lead your club.”
20–28s: Live match, tactical choice and scorecard/result. Caption: “Make the match yours.”
28–36s: Quick, genuine glimpses of coaching/recovery, scouting, facilities, Player Life and adviser. Caption: “Build beyond matchday.”
36–42s: Press/story moment, records and trophy or milestone from verified gameplay. Caption: “Write your cricket story.”
42–45s: Clean end card with the current icon and exact title “Cricket: Player and Manager”.

Editing rules: Let the video work when muted; add readable captions. Use only app-owned or properly licensed music, gentle cricket sound effects and smooth cuts. Show real gameplay within the first few seconds, with at least 80% of runtime representing the app experience. No artificial batting animation, invented tournament win, fabricated review, fake rating, store badge, price, “#1”, “Best”, or “Download now”. No phone frame. Remove emulator notices, QA tools, placeholder purchases and old “Cricket Legacy” branding. Keep any visual enhancement outside the actual app UI.

Before export, check the exact spelling of all text, image dimensions, screenshot order, video pacing, legibility at thumbnail size and fidelity to the current app. Deliver editable project files as well as final exports.
```
