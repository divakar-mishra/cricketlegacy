
## Full description

```text
One game. Two cricket careers. Your journey to the top.

Step into the life of a rising cricketer or take charge as a cricket manager. Develop your skills, make important decisions and build a career across seasons.

PLAYER CAREER

Start in Grade A cricket and work towards domestic and international selection. Choose your playing role, improve your attributes through training and earn your place through performances.

Build your reputation, explore sponsorships, upgrade your equipment and manage life beyond the boundary. An adviser helps you discover coaching, recovery and off-field opportunities as your career unfolds.

MANAGER CAREER

Take charge of a club and shape its future. Select your squad, plan training, manage transfers and make tactical decisions on matchday.

Develop your training ground, medical centre and youth academy. Improve your stadium, manage club finances and build a team capable of competing for trophies.

YOUR MATCHDAY, YOUR CHOICE

Follow the action, experience key moments or simulate matches. Review scorecards, track performances and adjust your approach for the next challenge.

BUILD YOUR CRICKET STORY

Navigate press conferences, career decisions and changing fortunes. Track records, celebrate milestones and see how your career is remembered.

Featuring fictional clubs and competitions, Cricket: Player and Manager brings the journey of a player and the decisions of a manager into one cricket game.

Will you make your name on the pitch or lead a club from the dugout?
```
