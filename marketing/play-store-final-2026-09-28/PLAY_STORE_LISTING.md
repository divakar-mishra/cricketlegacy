## Full description

```text
One game. Two cricket careers. Your decisions shape the journey.

Build a career as a cricketer or take charge of a club as its manager. Train, compete and make the choices that matter, from your next match to your next season.

PLAYER CAREER — EARN YOUR PLACE

Create your cricketer, choose a playing role and start in Grade A cricket. Develop your attributes through training and work towards youth, domestic and international opportunities. Selection depends on your performances: every appearance is a chance to make your case.

Manage form, fitness and injuries. Explore personal coaches, role-specific equipment, a performance analyst and physio support. A free career adviser offers timely suggestions; you decide whether to follow the advice or carry on.

MANAGER CAREER — LEAD YOUR CLUB

Select your squad and playing XI, appoint your leaders and prepare for matchday. Set tactics, arrange field positions and make decisions as the match unfolds.

Scout and recruit players, plan training and develop young prospects. Build your staff, improve the training ground and medical centre, and invest in your youth academy. Shape your home ground while keeping an eye on club finances.

MATCHDAY — MAKE YOUR CHOICES COUNT

Watch the action, play through key moments or use instant simulation. Follow the score, respond to the match situation and review detailed batting and bowling scorecards. Learn from each performance and prepare for the next challenge.

BEYOND THE BOUNDARY

Discover Player Life as your career grows. Explore sponsorships, kit choices, fictional investments and off-field ventures. Decide where to focus your resources, on and off the pitch. Investment values can rise or fall; these are in-game systems, not real-money investments.

BUILD A LASTING RECORD

Navigate press conferences and story decisions across seasons. Track career statistics, competition records, rankings, achievements and trophies. Follow the highs and setbacks that make your career your own.

Featuring fictional clubs and competitions, Cricket: Player & Manager brings the player's journey and the manager's decisions together in one cricket game.

Will you earn your place in the XI or build the team around it?
```
